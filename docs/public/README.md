# Mould Tracker user guide

Find a mould, check its location and condition, and keep its movement and maintenance records up to date.

## Start here

- [Sign in](getting-started/first-login.md).
- [Follow the quick start](getting-started/quick-start.md).
- [Browse the user guide](user-guide/README.md).
- [Get help with a problem](support/troubleshooting.md).

## Find what you need

- [Find a mould](user-guide/find-a-mould.md) by mould ID, product ID or description.
- [Find its storage position](user-guide/rack-view.md) on the rack layout.
- [Move a mould](user-guide/move-a-mould.md) and record where it went.
- [Record work](user-guide/work-log.md) or [report a problem](user-guide/report-a-problem.md).

The actions available to you depend on your assigned access. If an action is missing, ask your supervisor.
