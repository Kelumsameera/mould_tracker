# Troubleshooting

## Cannot sign in

Check your email address, password and network connection. Use the workplace app link. If the problem continues, contact your supervisor; do not share your password.

## Cannot find a mould

Clear the filters, check whether you are searching injection moulds or extrusion dies, and try the mould ID or product ID.

## Cannot move a mould

Read the displayed message. Check its condition, machine compatibility and whether the destination is available. Ask your supervisor if the required action is missing.

## Record changed while you were editing

Refresh the record and review its current location and condition before trying again. Another person may have already saved a change.

## App is offline

Check your workplace connection and reopen the app. Confirm the latest record before repeating a save.

## Ask for help

Provide the mould ID, the action you were trying to perform and the message shown on screen. Do not include passwords or other private account information.
