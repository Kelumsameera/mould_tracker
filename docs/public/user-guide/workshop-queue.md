# Workshop queue

Open **Workshop queue** to find moulds needing service or repair and check their current condition.

1. Select the mould you are working on.
2. Check its condition and recent work history.
3. Record the work performed and any required remarks.
4. Update its condition using the actions available to you.

Only mark a mould as good after the required work and checks are complete. A mould that has reached its service limit must complete the service process before returning to normal use.

When the mould is ready, record its next [movement](move-a-mould.md). See [Record work](work-log.md) for maintenance entries.
