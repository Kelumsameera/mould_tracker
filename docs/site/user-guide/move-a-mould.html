#Move a mould

  1. Find the mould and open its details.
  2. Choose the required destination from the available actions.
  3. Select the machine or storage position when requested.
  4. Add the required remarks and check the destination.
  5. Save the movement and confirm the displayed location.

#Before saving

  • Confirm that the mould ID matches the physical mould.
  • Use a compatible, available machine.
  • Choose an active, empty storage position. Each numbered position holds one mould.
  • Moulds requiring service or repair must be handled through the workshop process before normal use or storage.

The available destinations depend on your access and the mould's condition. If the app refuses a move, read its message and correct the issue or contact your supervisor.

See Find a storage position and Workshop queue.
