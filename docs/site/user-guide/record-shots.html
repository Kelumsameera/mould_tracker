#Record shots

  1. Open the mould that is currently mounted on the machine.
  2. Choose the action to record shots.
  3. Enter the additional shots produced, using a positive whole number.
  4. Check the amount and save.

Enter the number being added, not the mould's new total. Check the updated total after saving.

When the service limit is reached, the mould is flagged for service. Follow your workplace's service process before returning it to normal production.

If shots were entered incorrectly, contact your supervisor so the record can be reviewed.
