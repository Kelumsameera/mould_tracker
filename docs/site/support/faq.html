#FAQ

Frequently asked questions

👥 All usersVerifiedVerified 2026-10-29web/API 2.0.0

Why can't I put a mould from a rack straight onto a machine? That needs both storage and machine permissions, which only Admins and Super Admins have. On the floor, a Mould Maker brings it to the Production Floor and a Technician or Supervisor mounts it. See Mount and unmount.

Can I edit or delete a work-log entry? No. Log a correction entry instead; the old one is marked corrected.

Does reporting a problem stop the machine or change the status? No. It only flags the mould and lists it on the Workshop queue.

When is a problem closed? When someone logs work on the mould or sends it to the Workshop.

Is a deleted mould gone? No. It is hidden but keeps its history; an Admin can restore it.

Does the app work offline? It shows an offline page, but changes need the server.

Who can see the audit log? Super Admins and System Administrators.

Why does a name show as "Private user"? It belongs to a protected System Administrator account.

How often does the list refresh? Every 10 seconds.

Can I use Sinhala? Yes: the EN / සිං toggle. The choice follows your account.

Can I scan a QR code? No scanner is built in. Old #/…/m/<id> links still open the right mould.
