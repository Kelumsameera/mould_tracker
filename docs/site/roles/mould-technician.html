#Mould Technician (mould_technician) — default role for new users

Responsibilities and daily activities of the Mould Technician role

👥 Mould TechniciansVerified (permissions); daily routine is a suggestionVerified 2026-10-29web/API 2.0.0

#Can do

Machine moves (not from or to storage), send to the Workshop, change status at the Workshop, record shots, log work (with up to 4 co-workers), report problems.

#Cannot do

Move into or out of the Mould Room / Tool Room, override machine capability, or manage anything.

#Typical journey

Sign in → Workshop queue (flagged elsewhere) → mount / unmount on machines → Send to Workshop with remarks → log the work with outcome → sign out.
