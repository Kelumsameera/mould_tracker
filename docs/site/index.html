#Mould Tracker user guide

Find a mould, check its location and condition, and keep its movement and maintenance records up to date.

#Start here

  • Sign in.
  • Follow the quick start.
  • Browse the user guide.
  • Get help with a problem.

#Find what you need

  • Find a mould by mould ID, product ID or description.
  • Find its storage position on the rack layout.
  • Move a mould and record where it went.
  • Record work or report a problem.

The actions available to you depend on your assigned access. If an action is missing, ask your supervisor.
